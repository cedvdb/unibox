# Code of conduct

Unibox follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version
2.1. The short version of what that means here:

- **Be decent.** Critique the tree, the test or the diff — never the person who wrote it.
  "This renderer answers with its host's default, which is the drift we exist to prevent"
  is a review. "This is careless" is not.
- **Assume good faith and incomplete information.** Someone reporting that a token does
  the wrong thing has usually found a real gap; the answer may be that the token is not
  the one they wanted.
- **Keep the room usable.** No harassment, no personal attacks, no discriminatory
  language, no sustained disruption, in issues, pull requests, discussions or commits.

Reports go to the maintainer privately: use **Report abuse** on the comment or the
profile (GitHub's own reporting flow), and include a link to what happened. Reports are
read by the maintainer alone, and answered within a week.

What the maintainer may do, in proportion to the behaviour: ask for a change of tone, edit
or delete a comment, lock a thread, or — for harassment, or for someone who keeps going
after being asked to stop — block the account from the repository. A maintainer who is
part of a report does not decide on it alone.

The full text, including the enforcement ladder and the scope of the community, is the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
